# Original Song Concepts

Working titles and early directions for original Gruntry Crunge material.

---

### 1. 1/3 Eye Open
Possible theme: Half-awake, half-aware, living in the blur between clarity and haze.  
Mood: Mid-tempo, slightly paranoid, heavy groove.  
Vocal color: Closer to Tool / AIC side of the RowanShade range.

### 2. Switchin’ Eyes on the Highway
Theme: Restlessness, watching the road and the people in it, never quite landing.  
Mood: Driving song — literal and figurative. Organ + highway rhythm.  
Could sit near *I’m a Ramblin’ Man* energy.

### 3. Funny Shoe Song (working title)
Notes from dump: “Funny shoe song about slides reggae jimmy buffett whiskey”  
Direction: Light-hearted on the surface, slightly unhinged underneath. Reggae/Buffett lilt meets dirty southern rock. Slide guitar mandatory. Whiskey references welcome.

### 4. Hard to Handle
Classic title energy. Could be an original that feels like a lost Black Crowes / Skynyrd cut.  
Big chorus, greasy verses, organ punches.

### 5. Freight Train
Momentum song. Unstoppable, slightly dangerous.  
Heavy rhythm section, fiddle sawing against the beat.

### 6. Liquor Store
Late-night, fluorescent lights, bad decisions, and clarity that comes too late.  
Slow-to-mid tempo, piano + sparse dirty guitar.

### 7. Falling Out of Touch
Emotional distance, people drifting.  
Could lean more melodic / Neville-soul side of the voice with heavy undercurrent.

### 8. Familiar Faces
Seeing the same people in different places, or realizing you don’t really know them.  
Mid-tempo, observational, slightly haunted.

### 9. 10 Year Tear
Long slow heartbreak or long slow realization.  
Ballad territory — piano, fiddle, space. RowanShade voice at its most vulnerable.

---

## Development Priority (suggested)
1. **Switchin’ Eyes on the Highway** — strong title + clear energy
2. **1/3 Eye Open** — distinctive concept
3. **Liquor Store** or **10 Year Tear** — emotional anchors
4. **Funny Shoe Song** — needed for lighter / chaotic color in the set

Next step for any of these: write a full verse + chorus lyric draft + basic arrangement sketch.
